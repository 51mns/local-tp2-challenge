# Contributing a proof, counterexample or audit

Open an Issue or pull request. No access to the old private workspace is required.

## Counterexample

Provide the exact canonical word, the index n, degree-oriented S and D (or reproducible code), the four integer coefficients, and their product difference. A value of zero also refutes strict positivity. Explain how arithmetic, support and degree orientation were checked. Label noncanonical or auxiliary-condition witnesses explicitly.

## Proof

State the theorem and all assumptions. Identify inherited lemmas with public paths and exact versions. Separate finite calculations from the argument covering all words or parameters. Check strictness and the final supported index. A successful run of a checker is not a universal proof.

## Audit / useful partial result

Point to the exact manuscript, equation or program and the smallest reproducible failure. Distinguish an incorrect theorem from an insufficient bound, missing dependency or implementation error. Include enough detail to reproduce a negative result. You do not need to follow the existing research direction.

## Evidence and attribution

Disclose material AI assistance and which checks were actually executed. Do not describe two runs of the same model as external independent mathematical review. Submit only material you may publish; do not include credentials, private communications or unrelated personal data. Contributions retain their applicable path-specific licence; see LICENSE.

## Acceptance

Proposed proofs remain candidates until their assumptions and derivations have been reviewed. Please avoid claims such as 'solved', 'first' or 'formally verified' unless the linked evidence supports precisely that claim. Literature search failure is not evidence of novelty.
