# Supervisor audit and checkpoint scope

**Full-tree strict Local TP2 remains OPEN.** The mathematical progress in
this checkpoint is initialization of both regular first-level states,
an exact all-index relative-minor representation, and additional common
origin/child consequences. None of these is promoted to the missing
arbitrary regular-state changed-center or strict-proxy closure.

## Adopted common predicate

The old expanded predicate keeps the canonical algebra and ancestry,
four LR companions, both current paired packets, two certified origin
registers (including previous/current identities and small N>=2), and
strict proxy. The exact root is an exceptional clause for the LR links.

`audit_minimal_packet.md` proves that every ordinary origin and current
pair can instead use MP_sharp / MP2_exact: weak positive shifted traces,
strict supported single blocks in raw/y modes, strict seed degree, and
the exact mixed-character condition. H cones are derived, not assumed.
This retains CURRENT K_G, CURRENT K_M and the old P_Q target implication,
as well as LR and register BOTH transport. The root and both first-level
states satisfy even the old stronger uniform-4 packets.

The remaining assertions are therefore exactly the regular-state new
paired MP_sharp conditions, and the regular-state strict proxy. The root
edges' full packet initialization is no longer an open subcase. There is
no estimate here of the number of lemmas or attempts still required.

The supervisor derived the endpoint-1 residue certificates and supplied
the exact strict-times-weak proof used in the packet notes. That proof
requires deg(strict factor)>=deg(weak trace). It does not claim that
arbitrary strict-times-weak products are strict. For n>=1 choose the
Cauchy--Binet intermediate pair i,i+1 with i=max(h,min(n,f)); all folded
sum terms vanish and the weak factor contributes Delta_|i-n|>0. At n=0
its minor is 2h_h^2. Degree growth allows iteration through trace factors.

The first-level certificate was independently reconstructed using direct
Laurent arithmetic and the original scalar mutations. Every parameter
Bernstein array and supported defect is exact rational data. Complete
compressed certificates and their uncompressed SHA-256 hashes are saved.
Relative inequalities alone were NOT used to infer H cones when the
reference can have negative minors; H, L and trace cones are explicitly
certified in the finite bases.

## Scope safeguards

- The finite character theorem covers arbitrarily large kernel indices
  analytically; it is not an inference from a finite kernel window.
- A complete Bernstein certificate covers every real parameter in its
  stated domain. It is not a grid sample. Its finite STATE base does not
  establish all future states.
- Strict K_Q resolves the proxy outside the V overlap and at every
  terminal. The signed term W(Q,V) can be negative on actual states,
  including the saved two-long example. It is not discarded.
- The BOTH reverse L'_-1 result is one genuine parameter subgate.
  Neither other parameters nor changed-center mixed cross terms are
  silently supplied by it.
- The frozen 33-state/1,584-parameter-case probe found no failure, but
  it is not an induction or a continuum certificate. Its complete records
  are compressed and are not expanded into more optional searches.
- Negative power coefficients at the actual reversed smoothed root do
  not refute packet positivity: the full Bernstein certificate passes.
- The fixed-endpoint identity uses the correct bracket
  3yX+P(x-1)=3yX+x²+x-2, not 3yX+xP-2.
